# Video Background

Place your professional background video here as `background.mp4`.

## Recommended Specifications:
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
- **Duration**: 10-30 seconds (will loop)
- **File size**: Under 10MB for optimal performance
- **Content**: Professional tech/abstract animations, coding visuals, or elegant motion graphics

## Free Video Resources:
You can find professional background videos at:
- **Pexels Videos**: https://www.pexels.com/videos/
- **Pixabay**: https://pixabay.com/videos/
- **Coverr**: https://coverr.co/
